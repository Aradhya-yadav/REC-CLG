About College

Rajkiya Engineering College, Kannauj (RECK) is an institute committed to academic excellence and quality education. Established by the Government of Uttar Pradesh in 2015, RECK began its journey with a single branch, Electrical Engineering, admitting sixty (60) students under the affiliation of Harcourt Butler Technological University, Kanpur (formerly HBTI, Kanpur). In 2016, the college expanded by adding three more branches: Electronics Engineering, Civil Engineering, and Computer Science & Engineering, each with an annual intake of sixty (60) students. Currently, the institute is affiliated with Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, Lucknow (AKTU). Prof. (Dr.) M. Z. Khan joined as the founding director in April 2016, guiding the institution toward rapid growth. In July 2017, RECK moved from the HBTU campus to its own fully residential campus at Tirwa, Kannauj, where all academic activities have since been conducted.

The placement record at RECK is excellent, with an average package of 5–6 LPA, and students placed in reputed national and multinational companies. The college’s strong academic environment, combined with its focus on research, innovation, and industry collaboration, has rapidly positioned it as a rising star among engineering colleges in the region.

Strategically located just 15 km from Kannauj and 5 km from the Lucknow-Agra Expressway, the campus is well connected by road and rail, with direct connectivity to major cities like New Delhi, Kanpur, and Lucknow, making it accessible and well-placed for national-level academic and industry collaborations.
