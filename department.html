Departments

Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering offers a strong foundation in computing, programming, and AI. It provides students hands-on exposure to technologies such as machine learning, cloud computing, and web development.

Electronics Engineering

The Department of Electronics Engineering focuses on modern communication, signal processing, and embedded systems. It aims to prepare students for careers in semiconductor industries, IoT, and robotics.

Electrical Engineering

The Department of Electrical Engineering imparts knowledge of power systems, control engineering, and renewable energy technologies. The labs are equipped with advanced equipment for practical learning.

Civil Engineering

The Civil Engineering Department trains students in construction technology, structural design, environmental engineering, and surveying. Students learn through fieldwork and design projects for sustainable infrastructure.

Applied Science & Humanities

The Department of Applied Sciences lays the foundation of engineering by teaching physics, chemistry, mathematics, and communication skills, helping students to excel in technical as well as professional areas.
