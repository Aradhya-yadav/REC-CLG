Sport Club

Sports play a vital role in the overall development of students, promoting physical fitness, mental well-being, teamwork, and discipline. Our college recognizes the importance of sports and provides a wide range of facilities to encourage active participation among students. The campus is equipped with both indoor and outdoor sports infrastructure to cater to various interests and talents. We have well-maintained grounds and courts for popular outdoor games such as cricket, football, volleyball, kabaddi, kho-kho and athletics. For indoor sports enthusiasts, there are facilities for badminton, table tennis, chess, and carrom. Regular sports meets, tournaments, and inter-college competitions i.e. UTKRIDA (RECK Annual sports fest), APJAKTU Annual Sports fest etc. are organized to nurture sportsmanship and identify talented athletes. Overall, the sports culture in our college is vibrant and inclusive, providing students with ample opportunities to balance academics with physical activities and pursue excellence in both.

TECHNICAL CLUB

Introduction

In today’s rapidly evolving world, where technology plays a crucial role, technical clubs can be the essential platforms for fostering innovation, collaboration, and skill development among aspiring technologists. These clubs provide a vibrant environment where individuals with a passion for technology can come together to learn, share knowledge, and work on exciting projects. Rajkiya Engineering College Kannauj has opened a technical club with following key points:

Purpose and Objectives :

Our primary goal of opening technical club is to promote technical skills and providing a platform for enthusiasts to explore, experiment, and innovate. The core objectives of a technical club are: Knowledge Sharing: Encouraging to exchange the technical knowledge, ideas, and expertise among members through webinars, seminars, presentations, and discussions. Skill Development: Organizing training sessions, coding competitions, and hackathons to enhance technical skills and encourage problem-solving abilities. Project-based Learning: Encouraging members to work on real-world projects, developing practical application of concepts, and providing a hands-on learning experience. Networking and Collaboration: Facilitating networking opportunities with industry professionals, promoting collaborations with other clubs or institutions.

Organizational Structure :

Technical clubs typically have an organizational structure with some positions to ensure smooth functionality and effective management. Chairperson: Responsible for overall coordination, planning, and management of the club’s activities. Vice Chairperson: Assists the Chairperson and oversees specific club functions or departments. Secretary: Handles club communications, maintains records, and manages club documentation. Treasurer: Manages the club’s finances, budgeting, and fundraising activities. Social Media Coordinator: Handles social media management, club promotion, publicity, and outreach activities. Web Master: Promoting activities held in technical club on websites. Club members: Plan and organize various club activities, including workshops, competitions, and guest lectures.

Conclusion :

Thus, Technical club of Rajkiya Engineering College, Kannauj has been developed in order to create an inclusive and vibrant community that nurtures talent, sparks innovation, and cultivates a passion for technology. By providing a supportive environment for learning, collaboration, and practical experience, it plays a crucial role in shaping the future technologists and fostering the advancement of technology in various domains.
