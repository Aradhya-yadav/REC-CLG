Admission Process (JEECUP / JEE Main)

Admission to Rajkiya Engineering College, Kannauj is conducted through JEE Main and JEECUP counseling. The process is managed by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow.

  • Students must appear for JEE Main or JEECUP examination.
  • Participate in the online counseling process on the AKTU portal.
  • Select RECK Kannauj as your preferred choice during counseling.
  • Seats are allotted based on merit and category.
